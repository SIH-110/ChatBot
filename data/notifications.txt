DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE – NOTIFICATIONS

The Department of Justice publishes official notifications concerning matters under its administrative responsibility.

Notifications may relate to:

- Judicial appointments
- Appointment and transfer of judges
- Judicial service matters
- Court-related matters
- Government schemes
- Judicial infrastructure
- eCourts
- Fast Track Special Courts
- Access to Justice
- Administrative matters

A notification may contain:

- Notification number
- Date
- Subject
- Issuing authority
- Details of the decision or order
- Relevant instructions
- Attached official document

IMPORTANT

Notifications are official government documents and may have legal or administrative significance.

The latest notification and its original attached document should always be consulted before relying on the information.
