DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE – CIRCULARS

The Department of Justice publishes official circulars relating to administrative, judicial and departmental matters.

Circulars may contain instructions, guidelines, procedures or clarifications issued by the Department.

Subjects may include:

- Judicial administration
- Court-related matters
- Judicial appointments
- Service matters
- Judicial reforms
- eCourts
- Access to Justice
- Fast Track Special Courts
- Administrative procedures

A circular may contain:

- Circular number
- Date
- Subject
- Issuing authority
- Instructions
- Guidelines
- Reference documents
- Attached PDF documents

IMPORTANT

Circulars may be amended, superseded or withdrawn. The latest official circular should therefore be checked before relying on its contents.
