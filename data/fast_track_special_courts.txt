FAST TRACK SPECIAL COURTS (FTSCs)

The Fast Track Special Courts (FTSCs) scheme is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme implemented by the Government of India through the Department of Justice, Ministry of Law and Justice.

OBJECTIVE

The Fast Track Special Courts scheme is intended to provide speedy and time-bound disposal of pending cases relating to rape and offences under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012.

The scheme was launched in October 2019.

The courts are dedicated to dealing with cases involving rape and offences under the POCSO Act.

EXCLUSIVE POCSO COURTS

The scheme also provides for exclusive POCSO (e-POCSO) Courts.

These courts are dedicated to the trial and disposal of cases relating to offences under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012.

SCHEME EXTENSION

The Fast Track Special Courts scheme has been extended twice.

The latest extension was approved up to 31 March 2026 for the establishment of 790 Fast Track Special Courts.

FUNCTIONAL COURTS

According to information provided by the High Courts, as of 31 January 2026:

- 774 Fast Track Special Courts were functional.
- 398 of these were exclusive POCSO (e-POCSO) Courts.
- The functional courts were spread across 29 States and Union Territories.

DISPOSAL OF CASES

As of 31 January 2026, Fast Track Special Courts had collectively disposed of 3,71,849 cases since the inception of the scheme.

FUNDING

The Fast Track Special Courts have been set up and operationalised under the Nirbhaya Fund.

The Department of Justice has released approximately Rs. 1,210.91 crore to States and Union Territories since the inception of the scheme to support the functioning of these courts.

The funding pattern is based on the Centrally Sponsored Scheme model.

The Central and State funding ratio is generally:

- 60:40 for applicable States
- 90:10 for North Eastern and certain special category States

The funding supports the salary of one Judicial Officer, seven support staff and a flexi grant for day-to-day expenses.

RESPONSIBILITY FOR SETTING UP COURTS

The setting up and functioning of Fast Track Special Courts falls within the domain of the respective State Governments and Union Territories in consultation with their High Courts.

The establishment of courts depends on the requirements and availability of resources of the concerned State or Union Territory.

The information regarding functional courts is provided by the respective High Courts.

CURRENT DASHBOARD INFORMATION

The Department of Justice Fast Track Special Courts dashboard provides live information about the functioning of FTSCs.

The dashboard currently reports:

The dashboard figures are dynamic and may change as States and High Courts update their information.

IMPORTANCE

Fast Track Special Courts are intended to reduce delays in the trial of serious offences involving rape and children.

The scheme supports dedicated judicial capacity for these cases and aims to provide faster justice to victims.

KEY FACTS

Scheme: Fast Track Special Courts (FTSCs)

Implemented by: Department of Justice, Ministry of Law and Justice, Government of India

Type: Centrally Sponsored Scheme

Launched: October 2019

Main cases: Rape and offences under the POCSO Act, 2012

Latest approved scheme period: Up to 31 March 2026

Approved number of courts: 790

Functional courts as reported on 31 January 2026: 774

Exclusive POCSO courts as reported on 31 January 2026: 398

States/UTs with functional FTSCs as reported on 31 January 2026: 29

Cases disposed since inception as reported on 31 January 2026: 3,71,849

Funding source: Nirbhaya Fund

NOTE ON LIVE DATA

The Department of Justice maintains a live dashboard for Fast Track Special Courts. Dashboard figures can change when updated information is received from High Courts.

Therefore, when answering questions about the current number of functional FTSCs, the latest Department of Justice dashboard figure should be preferred over older dated figures.
