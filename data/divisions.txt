DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE – DIVISIONS AND ORGANISATIONAL FUNCTIONS

The Department of Justice is part of the Ministry of Law and Justice, Government of India. It is headed by the Minister of Law and Justice, while the Secretariat is headed by the Secretary (Justice).

The Department handles matters relating to judicial administration, appointment and service matters of judges, judicial reforms, court infrastructure, computerisation of courts, access to justice and legal aid.

ORGANISATIONAL SETUP

The Department of Justice has a Secretariat headed by Secretary (Justice). The organisational setup includes senior officers such as Additional Secretaries, Joint Secretaries, Deputy Secretaries/Directors and Under Secretaries.

The Department's work is distributed among different sections/divisions dealing with specific areas of justice administration.

MAJOR AREAS / DIVISIONS OF WORK

1. JUDICIAL APPOINTMENTS

This area deals with matters concerning the appointment, resignation and removal of:

- Chief Justice of India
- Judges of the Supreme Court of India
- Chief Justices of High Courts
- Judges of High Courts

It also deals with their salaries, leave, pensions, travelling allowances and other service matters.

2. HIGH COURT AND SUPREME COURT MATTERS

The Department deals with matters concerning the constitution and organisation of the Supreme Court and High Courts, subject to the allocation of subjects under the Government of India Allocation of Business Rules.

3. JUDICIAL INFRASTRUCTURE

The Department facilitates development and improvement of infrastructure for courts, particularly subordinate courts.

This includes schemes and programmes intended to provide better facilities for judicial officers, court staff and citizens using the courts.

4. eCOURTS AND ICT ENABLEMENT

The Department supports computerisation and digitalisation of courts through the eCourts Project.

The objective is to use information and communication technology to improve judicial services, provide digital access to court information and make court processes more efficient.

5. JUDICIAL REFORMS

The Department works on policies and programmes relating to judicial reforms and improvement of justice delivery.

The objective is to improve the efficiency, accessibility and timeliness of the justice system.

6. ACCESS TO JUSTICE

The Department facilitates programmes intended to improve access to justice, particularly for people who face difficulties in accessing legal and judicial services.

7. LEGAL AID

The Department supports initiatives for providing legal aid to poor and disadvantaged persons, including implementation of programmes under the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987.

8. FAST TRACK SPECIAL COURTS

The Department facilitates schemes for speedy disposal of specified categories of cases through dedicated courts, including the Fast Track Special Courts scheme for cases involving rape and offences under the POCSO Act.

9. NATIONAL JUDICIAL ACADEMY

The Department supports the National Judicial Academy and programmes related to training and capacity building of judicial officers.

10. COURTS IN UNION TERRITORIES

The Department deals with matters relating to administration of justice and constitution and organisation of courts in Union Territories, along with related court fees and subjects assigned under the Allocation of Business Rules.

11. ALL INDIA JUDICIAL SERVICE

The Department handles matters relating to creation and development of the All India Judicial Service.

12. JUDICIAL OFFICERS

The Department deals with conditions of service of District Judges and other members of the Higher Judicial Service in Union Territories.

SUMMARY

The Department of Justice distributes its responsibilities across several major areas including judicial appointments, court administration, judicial infrastructure, eCourts and ICT, judicial reforms, access to justice, legal aid, Fast Track Special Courts, judicial training and matters relating to courts and judicial officers.

These areas collectively support the Department's objective of improving justice delivery and facilitating easier and timely access to justice.
