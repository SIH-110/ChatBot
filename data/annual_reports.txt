DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE – ANNUAL REPORTS

The Department of Justice is a Department under the Ministry of Law and Justice, Government of India.

The Annual Reports of the Department of Justice provide information about the organisation, functions, programmes, schemes and activities of the Department.

The Annual Reports cover matters including:

1. Judicial appointments
2. Administration of justice
3. Judicial infrastructure
4. Computerisation of courts
5. eCourts Project
6. Fast Track Special Courts
7. Access to Justice
8. Legal aid
9. Judicial reforms
10. Digitalisation of judicial services

The Annual Reports also provide information concerning the Supreme Court, High Courts, District and Subordinate Courts and various schemes administered or monitored by the Department of Justice.

The Annual Report 2022-23 contains information about the organisation and functions of the Department and its activities during the reporting period.

The Department of Justice is responsible for matters allocated to it under the Government of India (Allocation of Business) Rules.
