ACTS AND RULES – DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

The Department of Justice is responsible for various matters relating to the administration of justice and courts under the Government of India (Allocation of Business) Rules.

Subjects associated with the Department include:

- Constitution and organisation of the Supreme Court.
- Constitution and organisation of High Courts.
- Appointment, resignation and removal of the Chief Justice of India and Judges of the Supreme Court.
- Appointment, resignation and removal of Chief Justices and Judges of High Courts.
- Appointment of Judicial Commissioners and Judicial Officers in Union Territories.
- Administration of justice in Union Territories.
- Creation of an All India Judicial Service.
- Conditions of service of District Judges and members of Higher Judicial Service in Union Territories.
- Judicial reforms and related matters.
