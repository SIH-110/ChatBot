ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE

The Department of Justice (DoJ) is a part of the Ministry of Law & Justice, Government of India. It is one of the oldest Ministries/Departments of the Government of India.

ORGANIZATIONAL BACKGROUND

Till 31 December 2009, the Department of Justice was part of the Ministry of Home Affairs and the Union Home Secretary was the Secretary of the Department of Justice.

Keeping in view the increasing workload and the need for formulating policies and programmes relating to judicial reforms in the country, a separate Department of Justice was carved out from the Ministry of Home Affairs. It was placed under the charge of a Secretary to the Government of India under the Ministry of Law & Justice and started functioning as a separate Department from January 2010.

The Department of Justice is housed at Jaisalmer House, 26, Man Singh Road, New Delhi.

FUNCTIONS OF THE DEPARTMENT

The Department of Justice is responsible for matters relating to the appointment, resignation and removal of:

- The Chief Justice of India
- Judges of the Supreme Court of India
- Chief Justices of the High Courts
- Judges of the High Courts

The Department also deals with service matters relating to judges of the higher judiciary.

Other important functions and programmes of the Department include:

- Development of infrastructure facilities for the judiciary.
- Setting up of Special Courts for speedy trial and disposal of cases of sensitive nature.
- Implementation of the Fast Track Special Courts scheme for cases relating to rape and offences under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act.
- Implementation of the eCourts Project for computerisation and digitalisation of courts across the country.
- Providing legal aid to poor and disadvantaged persons.
- Promoting access to justice.
- Providing financial assistance to the National Judicial Academy for training judicial officers.
- Implementing policies and programmes relating to judicial reforms.

The functions of the Department of Justice are specified in the Allocation of Business Rules, 1961.

VISION

The vision of the Department of Justice is:

"Facilitating administration of Justice that ensures easy access and timely delivery of Justice to all."

MISSION

The mission of the Department of Justice includes:

- Ensuring adequacy of courts and judges.
- Facilitating appointments of judges to the higher judiciary.
- Modernisation of courts and judicial procedures.
- Formulation and implementation of policies for judicial reforms.
- Providing legal aid to the poor.
- Improving the overall delivery of justice.

ORGANISATIONAL SETUP

The Department of Justice forms part of the Ministry of Law and Justice.

The Department is headed by the Minister of Law and Justice.

The Secretariat of the Department is headed by the Secretary (Justice).

The organisational setup includes senior officers and officials responsible for different areas of justice administration, judicial reforms, court infrastructure, eCourts, legal aid and access to justice.

IMPORTANT AREAS OF WORK

The major areas of work of the Department of Justice include:

1. Appointment and service matters of judges of the Supreme Court and High Courts.
2. Judicial infrastructure development.
3. Judicial reforms.
4. Computerisation and digitalisation of courts.
5. eCourts Project.
6. Fast Track Special Courts.
7. Legal aid.
8. Access to justice.
9. Training and capacity building of judicial officers.
10. Technology-enabled services for citizens and the judiciary.

DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE AND DIGITAL JUSTICE

The Department of Justice supports the use of information and communication technology in the Indian judicial system through initiatives such as the eCourts Project.

The eCourts initiative aims to improve access to judicial information and services, computerise courts, provide digital services to litigants and lawyers, and make information relating to cases more easily accessible.

The National Judicial Data Grid (NJDG) provides information relating to pending and disposed cases and is an important component of the technology-enabled judicial system.

LEGAL AID AND ACCESS TO JUSTICE

The Department of Justice works towards improving access to justice, particularly for poor and disadvantaged sections of society.

Its programmes include initiatives for legal aid, access to justice and the use of technology to make judicial and legal services more accessible to citizens.

FAST TRACK SPECIAL COURTS

The Department of Justice implements the Fast Track Special Courts scheme for expeditious disposal of cases relating to rape and offences under the POCSO Act.

The scheme is intended to support speedy trial and disposal of such cases through dedicated courts.

SUMMARY

The Department of Justice is a department under the Ministry of Law and Justice, Government of India. It plays an important role in judicial administration, appointment and service matters of higher judiciary, judicial infrastructure, judicial reforms, eCourts, Fast Track Special Courts, legal aid and access to justice.

Its overall objective is to facilitate easy access to justice and timely delivery of justice to citizens.
