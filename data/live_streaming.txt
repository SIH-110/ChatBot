SUPREME COURT OF INDIA – LIVE STREAMING

The Supreme Court of India provides a Live Streaming section on its official website.

The Live Streaming page provides access to previous court proceedings of:

1. Constitution Bench Matters
2. Ceremonial Bench proceedings

Users can access previous proceedings through the official ARCHIVE link available on the Live Streaming page.

Official Website:
https://www.sci.gov.in/

Live Streaming Page:
https://www.sci.gov.in/live-streaming/

Archive:
The Live Streaming page contains an official ARCHIVE link for previous Constitution Bench Matters and Ceremonial Bench proceedings.

IMPORTANT

The availability of live streaming depends on the proceedings and applicable directions of the Supreme Court.

The Supreme Court website also provides other judicial services including:

- Cause List
- Case Status
- Daily Orders
- Judgments
- e-Filing
- Online Appearance Slips
- Certified Copy
- National Judicial Data Grid
- Verdict Finder
